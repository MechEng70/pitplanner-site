Pit Planner

Cook-day planning for smokers and griddles.

Tell it what you're cooking and when you want to eat. Pit Planner works backward and tells you when each piece goes on, when to check it, and when it comes off, so the brisket, the ribs, and the sides all land at the same time.

Get it on Google Play Buy me a bag of pellets

Free, Android only, US only for now. In closed testing; the Play link goes live at public release.

What it does

  • Plans the whole cook so every item finishes on time, across smokers and griddles.
  • Starts with hours-per-pound timing, then learns from your own cooks.
  • Fahrenheit or Celsius.
  • Share a plan with someone else cooking with you.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Your plans stay on your phone.
