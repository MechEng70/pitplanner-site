Privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Pit Planner is a free Android app published on Google Play by Jason Glithero (developer name MechEng70). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. The short version: Pit Planner doesn't collect, sell, or share your data.

What the app stores

Your cook plans, settings, and cook history are saved only on your phone, in the app's private storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind.
  • No ads or advertising IDs.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo access.
  • No data sold or shared with third parties.

Things you choose to do

  • Sharing a plan. When you tap Share, the app hands the plan to Android's share sheet and you pick where it goes (text, email, and so on). Whatever app you choose handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Importing a plan. When you open a plan file, the app reads only the file you pick.
  • Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include Pit Planner's data in your own Google backup. That backup is controlled by your Google account and Google's privacy policy; we can't see it.
  • "Buy me a bag of pellets." This link opens Buy Me a Coffee in your browser. Anything you do there is between you and Buy Me a Coffee and is covered by their privacy policy. The app doesn't send them anything.

Future features

A planned optional Home Assistant connection would talk only to the Home Assistant server address you enter, directly from your phone. It is not in the current version. If a future version changes what the app does with data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Children

Pit Planner is meant for adults who cook and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Everything lives on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it all.

Questions about this policy: pitplanapp@gmail.com.
